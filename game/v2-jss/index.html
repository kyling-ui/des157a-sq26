How to Play

Objective: Build the biggest (or smallest) equation using the dice rolls.

Steps

1. Press 'Start Game' to roll the dice. Six of the same numbers will be dealt to both players.

2. Player 1 goes first. Place an operator between each of your six numbers. You can use addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, or exponents.

3. Once Player 1 is happy with their equation, press 'Lock In' on Player 1's side. (Player 1 can unlock their equation, but once Player 2 locks in their equation, the round is over).

4. After Player 1 has locked in, Player 2 can place their own operators. Player 2 should press 'Lock In' on their side when readdy.

5. Once both players are locked in, both equations are calculated automatically. TThe player with the bigger (or smaller) result wins the round, depending on the game setting.

6. Each win adds to the player's win count. If both equations are tied, neither player is awarded points.

Operators: '+' = Add, '-' = Subtract, *' = Multiply, '/' = Divide, '**' = Power / Exponential
